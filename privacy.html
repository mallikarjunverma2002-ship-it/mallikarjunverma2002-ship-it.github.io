Privacy Policy

OpenClaw Personal Assistant - last updated 18 November 2026

Who uses this app

This app is for personal use by its owner only. It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.

What data it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's own Google Calendar, Google Tasks and Gmail through Google's official APIs, only to carry out the owner's requests (for example, creating a reminder, adding a to-do item, or reading and sending an email).

Where data is stored

Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's secure credential store. The app has no servers of its own and does not upload Google data anywhere other than back to Google when performing a requested action.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is not used to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
